Three minutes to set up. Then it's just texts.

  1. 1

    Connect mail and calendar

    Read-only. Your inbox shows what you already love: the bands you've bought tickets for, the cinema newsletters you actually open. Your calendar shows when you're free.

  2. 2

    Set a budget

    A monthly limit, and a smaller amount it may spend without asking. Say €100 a month, and surprises up to €25.

  3. 3

    Say yes

    When it finds something, it texts you. Reply yes and it books. If it's under your surprise limit and it's sure you'll love it, it books first and tells you after.

Your limits are rules, not suggestions
The AI picks the events. A plain, fixed rule decides whether money moves: inside your surprise limit it books, above it asks, past your monthly budget it stops.
The price is checked at checkout
If the total on the payment page is higher than the price it quoted you, it doesn't pay. It texts you instead.
It never sees your card number
Payment details are filled in by the browser at the last step. The model only ever knows the last four digits.
