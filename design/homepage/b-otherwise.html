    Set up once, in about three minutes.

    1. Connect mail and calendar

      Gmail or Outlook, read-only. Your ticket receipts and the newsletters you open say what you like. Your calendar says when you're free.

    2. Set your two numbers

      A monthly budget, and how much it may spend without asking. You can change both by text at any time.

    3. Get your first plan

      Within a day it texts you something worth leaving the house for. Reply yes, no, or "more like this".

    Careful with your inbox and your card.

    Reads, never sends
    Mail access is read-only. It looks for tickets, bookings and newsletters, and never writes from your account.
    Checks before it pays
    If the checkout total is higher than the price it showed you, it stops and asks.
    Never sees your card
    Card details are filled in at the last step by the browser. The AI only knows the last four digits.
    Puts it in your calendar
    Everything it books lands in your calendar with the venue, doors time and tickets attached.
