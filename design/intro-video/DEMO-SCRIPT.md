About 400 words of voiceover, which takes roughly 2:45 at a calm pace and leaves room to breathe. Timings are targets;
the edit follows the recordings.

Footage: **[INTRO]** the Remotion intro · **[WEB]** website scroll recording · **[PHONE]** onboarding + WhatsApp
recording (Twilio avatar and name replaced with the capybara and "Otherwise") · **[SETTINGS]** settings recording ·
**[GFX]** Remotion graphics (captions, callouts, zooms).

## Optional 15 s "how it's built" beat (for judges), between 2:25 and 2:45 if we trim settings

"Under the hood: a Mastra agent on Neon, Exa to find events, Kernel browsers to book them, and a fixed rule in code,
not the model, that decides book, ask, or skip." Picture: [GFX] the five logos on the paper background, then the

## Recording checklist (phone)

- Before recording, save the Twilio sandbox number in Contacts as **Otherwise**. Then WhatsApp may show that name
  instead of the number, which leaves less to cover. The avatar still has to be replaced in the edit.
- Turn on Do Not Disturb (no notification banners over the chat). Use the same light/dark mode for the whole recording,
  and record at the phone's native resolution.
- Don't show the sandbox "join …" message or the full phone number, or tell us the timestamps so we can cut or blur them.
- Hold still for about 2 s on each key screen (setup link arriving, each WhatsApp message, the "Booked" message) so the
  edit can zoom in.
- Note the timestamps of the beats in the table above. That makes the cut much faster.
